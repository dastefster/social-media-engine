02 · Features

Everything you need to
own your feed.

One script. Four core tools. All running natively in Scriptable.

👥
Follower Tracker
Snapshot your followers list and compare it against the last run — new followers and unfollowers shown instantly.
📊
Followers History Graph
Beautiful HTML chart generated on demand — visualize your follower growth over time, opened directly in Safari.
🔔
Change Log
Persistent log of every follow and unfollow event with timestamps. Full history, never overwritten.
⬇️
Media Downloader
Download posts, reels, carousels, and stories at full quality. Share a link from Instagram and it handles the rest.
🎬
VP9 Video Conversion
Instagram reels use VP9 encoding which iOS can't save to Photos. SME automatically converts them to HEVC or H.264 via a-Shell mini.
🔐
Secure Session Storage
Auth credentials stored only in local Scriptable storage — never in iCloud, never displayed, never sent anywhere.
03 · How it works

Follower tracking,
on autopilot.

Scriptable does the heavy lifting. Shortcuts just pulls the trigger.

1
First-Time Setup
Run the shortcut once. A guided setup walks you through logging into Instagram via secure WebView — your session is cached locally.
2
Run Anytime
Launch from the main Shortcuts app, your home screen widget, or share sheet. The script picks up right where it left off.
3
Followers Are Fetched
Your current followers list is pulled via Instagram's private API and compared to your last cached snapshot automatically.
4
See What Changed
New followers and unfollowers are displayed with profile links. Tap any account to open their profile in Safari.
5
History Updates
Every run logs your follower count. Open the history graph anytime to see your growth over time as a clean visual chart.
03b · Downloading media

Three ways to
grab content.

Photos, reels, carousels — any public post, your way.

1
Get the Link In
Three ways to trigger a download: tap Share in Instagram and select Social Media Engine from the share sheet; copy a post URL to your clipboard and run the shortcut (it detects it automatically); or paste a URL directly from the Download option in the main menu.
2
Media Is Resolved
The script fetches the full-quality media URLs directly from Instagram's API — no third-party servers, no compression.
3
Pick What You Want
For carousel posts, a selection screen lets you choose exactly which photos and videos to save. Select one, a few, or all.
4
VP9 Videos Auto-Convert
Instagram reels use VP9 encoding which iOS can't save to Photos. If detected, SME automatically converts them to HEVC or H.264 via a-Shell mini — no manual steps needed.
5
Saved Your Way
Content lands in Photos, Files, or the share sheet — wherever you configured in Settings. Captions can be copied to clipboard automatically too.

05 · Before You Begin

A few things to
know first.

Read these before running for the first time.

⚠️
Two-Factor Authentication
If you have 2FA enabled on your Instagram account, you may need to temporarily disable it during the initial login to allow the script to cache your session. Once setup is complete you can re-enable 2FA — the cached session persists until it expires naturally.
🔒
Your Data Stays on Your Device
Your Instagram session tokens and credentials are stored exclusively in local Scriptable storage on your device. They are never written to iCloud, never transmitted to any external server, and are never displayed or accessible outside the script itself.
📥
Download Function Credit
The media download functionality is based on the work of @gluebyte on RoutineHub. Huge credit to them for the original Instagram Download shortcut.
